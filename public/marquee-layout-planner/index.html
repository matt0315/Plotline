What size marquee do I need?

A quick guide using common planning densities: 1.2 m² per guest for a seated dinner, 0.7 m² for theatre rows, and 0.5 m² for a standing cocktail event. Leave room for the dance floor, bar, buffet and stage. Plotline works out the exact number for your layout.

Questions

How many guests fit in a 10 × 20 m marquee?

About 166 for a seated dinner (1.2 m² each), 285 in theatre rows, or 400 standing for cocktails, before the dance floor, bar and stage. Lay it out in Plotline for the exact number with your tables.

Can I plan a tipi or stretch tent?

Yes. Tipis, joined tipis, stretch tents, sailcloth, pole, frame and clearspan marquees are all included, each with its own pole layout.

I run a marquee hire business. Can I use my own stock?

Yes. Add your marquees and furniture with sizes, prices and quantities owned. Plans built with them show the hire cost and any shortfall, and you can send clients a clean PDF.
