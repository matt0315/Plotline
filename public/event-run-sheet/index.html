Questions

What should an event run sheet include?

Times for set-up, the event itself and pack-down; what happens at each time; who’s responsible; and where. Supplier arrivals, sound checks and venue handover are the items most often forgotten.

Can I start from a template?

Yes. Pick wedding, corporate, festival or party and you get a full draft to edit, with times based on your start time.

Can my crew see the run sheet on the day?

On Pro you can share a live link that always shows the latest version, and crew can fill in your forms from a link or QR code.

Is it free?

Yes. The free plan includes every planning feature for one event, saved in your browser, with no signup. Exports carry a small watermark. Pro (US$39/month) adds unlimited events, clean exports, sync across devices and live share links for clients and crew.
