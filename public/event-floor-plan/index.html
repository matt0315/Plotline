Questions

Is the floor plan really to scale?

Yes. Everything is drawn in metres (or feet), from table diameters and chair spacing to stage decks, so what fits on screen fits in the room.

Can I use the venue’s CAD drawing?

Yes. Import a DXF, a PDF or a photo of the plan, set two points of known distance, and it becomes the background you design on.

Does it check fire exits and walkways?

It flags walkways under 915 mm, items blocking an exit, and too little exit width for the number of people (5 mm per person, with at least two exits over 50 people). It’s a planning guide: your venue licence and local code decide the real numbers.

Do I need to install anything for 3D?

No. Plotline runs in your browser on a laptop, tablet or phone, including the 3D view.

Is it free?

Yes. The free plan includes every planning feature for one event, saved in your browser, with no signup. Exports carry a small watermark. Pro (US$39/month) adds unlimited events, clean exports, sync across devices and live share links for clients and crew.
